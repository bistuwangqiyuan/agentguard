## 2. Per-archetype findings

### Archetype 1: API-as-a-product (screenshot API, PDF API, data extraction API)

**Named examples:**
- **ScreenshotOne** — Dmytro Krasun, solo. Launched May 2022 → $10K MRR Sept 2024 (**~2.5 years**) → **$20K MRR June 2025**, $200K ARR with 400+ paying customers [SR, open milestones]. His own warning: *"Screenshot APIs is a bad example. Cause there is not a lot of money, but harsh competition."* Also complains MRR-sharing attracts copycats. ([screenshotone.com blog](https://screenshotone.com/blog/200000-arr-and-400-paying-customers/), [newsletter](https://newsletter.dmytrokrasun.com/p/reached-20k-mrr-whats-next))
- **Bannerbear** (image/PDF generation API) — Jon Yongfook, solo. 18 months to $10K MRR; **$1M ARR Sept 2025 after 6 years** [SR, open page]. Grew via "50/50 split": one week code, one week content/free tools. ([bannerbear.com/journey-to-10k-mrr](https://www.bannerbear.com/journey-to-10k-mrr/), [ideaindex.so](https://www.ideaindex.so/case-studies/bannerbear))
- **ScrapingBee** (web scraping API) — 2 founders (Pierre de Wulf, Kevin Sahin). 18 months to $10K MRR, $1M ARR in 2.5 years, **$5M ARR → 8-figure all-cash exit to Oxylabs, June 2025** [V — acquisition press release]. Note: they **deliberately killed their $9 and $29 plans** because cheap customers generated disproportionate support. ([scrapingbee.com/journey-to-one-million-arr](https://www.scrapingbee.com/journey-to-one-million-arr/), [accessnewswire](https://www.accessnewswire.com/newsroom/en/computers-technology-and-internet/oxylabs-company-group-acquires-one-of-the-leading-scraping-compan-1039844))

**Support burden: LOW (best of all archetypes).** Customers are developers; docs deflect most tickets; no "how do I use this" tier if docs are good. Qualitative consensus: a few tickets/week at $10–20K MRR. Caveat (ScrapingBee): bottom-tier hobbyist customers generate most tickets — price floor of $49+/mo largely eliminates them.
**Distribution automatability: HIGH.** SEO on "X API" keywords + programming-language tutorial content + free developer tools. Bannerbear and ScreenshotOne grew ~100% via SEO/content with zero ads.
**Pricing/WTP:** $29–$149/mo typical tiers; usage-based expansion. Developers/businesses pay reliably; churn is low once embedded in production code (real switching cost — code rewrite).
**Time to first revenue / ceiling:** SLOW — consistently ~18 months to $10K MRR even for skilled founders. Ceiling for solo: $20K MRR (ScreenshotOne, 3 yrs) to $1M ARR (Bannerbear, 6 yrs).
**Failure modes:** commoditization (dozens of screenshot APIs), price-race-to-bottom, copycats cloning your public roadmap, big-cloud free tiers, abuse/fraud of free trials.
**2026 saturation:** generic categories (screenshot, HTML→PDF, scraping) are crowded; niche data-extraction verticals less so. AI agents consuming APIs is a genuine new demand tailwind.

### Archetype 2: Vertical AI micro-SaaS (one profession/vertical)

**Named examples:**
- **LaunchFast** (Amazon FBA product research, AI) — Hassam, non-technical, built with Cursor in 48h; **~$20–25K MRR within 90 days (2025)** [SR via Starter Story video — promotional context, treat as upper bound]. Crucial detail: he **traded equity to a coaching community (Legacy X) for instant distribution** to his exact niche. ([starterstory.com/hassam](https://www.starterstory.com/hassam))
- **ChatSEO** (AI SEO assistant for founders/agencies) — Paul Vengeons (freelance SEO consultant). **€14K MRR in ~3 months, €70K in first 5 months, zero ads** [SR]. Productized his own consulting expertise; 8-channel organic system (LinkedIn playbooks, YouTube, free micro-tools). ([mrrstory.com](https://www.mrrstory.com/stories/how-chatseo-grew-to-14k-mrr-using-only-organic-traffic))
- **ABConvert** (A/B price testing for Shopify — vertical SaaS on a marketplace) — Jeffrey Lin: $1K MRR in 5 months → **$50K MRR in 24 months**, multi-million acquisition offer [SR]. ([jeff0723.medium.com](https://jeff0723.medium.com/how-to-build-a-shopify-app-worth-1m-in-24-months-f688c1313300))

**Support burden: MEDIUM.** Niche professionals expect responsiveness, but a narrow workflow = small surface area. Per Groundwork's synthesis of public founder accounts: **5–15 tickets/week between $1K–$25K MRR, 5–10 h/week founder time**; a 50–100 article KB deflects 40–60% ([groundworkblog.com](https://groundworkblog.com/articles/micro-saas-customer-support-solo-founder), [happysupport.ai](https://happysupport.ai/blog/self-service-rate-support-metrics)).
**Distribution:** Word-of-mouth inside tight niches is the strongest channel (the $2K→$50K MRR case attributed acceleration entirely to in-niche WOM, after **five failed generic products** — [builtwithagents.ai](https://www.builtwithagents.ai/strategy/ai-saas-2k-to-50k-mrr-8-months-niche)). But it requires *being in or buying into the niche community* — semi-automatable at best.
**Pricing/WTP:** $29–99/mo; professionals pay for tools tied to income (Amazon sellers, SEO agencies, e-com merchants are proven payers).
**Time to first revenue:** fast (weeks–months) *if you have domain knowledge + a channel into the niche*; otherwise slow.
**Failure modes:** picking a niche you don't belong to (no distribution, no insight); niche too small; platform dependency; the "thin wrapper" trap if the AI does nothing a ChatGPT prompt can't.
**2026 saturation:** the *strategy* is now canonical advice, so obvious niches (SEO, Amazon, real estate copy) are crowded; obscure profession niches remain open. Vertical SaaS is repeatedly cited as the most defensible micro-SaaS category in 2026 ([startupa.ge analysis](https://startupa.ge/blog/micro-saas-ideas-2026)).

### Archetype 3: Self-serve AI generation tools (headshots, documents, transcription/subtitles)

**Named examples:** PhotoAI ($132–138K/mo [SR]), HeadshotPro (~$300K/mo [SR]) — see §1.2. Plus:
- **Submagic** (AI subtitles/short-video) — David Zitoun + Tsi-fei Chan (2 founders, France). $1M ARR in **90 days** (Aug 2023) → **$8M ARR, 13 employees, 2025** [SR/Latka]. Growth: TikTok virality + **10,000+ affiliate army** (affiliates drive ~$1.6M of ARR at 30% lifetime commission). ([getlatka.com/companies/submagic.co](https://getlatka.com/companies/submagic.co), [timfrin.substack.com](https://timfrin.substack.com/p/inside-submagics-journey-to-8m-arr))
- **Formula Bot** (Excel/data AI) — David Bressler, solo, non-programmer, built on Bubble. ~$24K/mo at peak 2023, **$500K ARR / ~$42K mo (2024-25), >$1M lifetime sales, 1M users** [SR + Bubble case study]. Growth: exact-match domain (excelformulabot.com) + **free-tool SEO** ("PDF to Excel converter", "Excel formula generator"). ([bubble.io/blog/formula-bot](https://bubble.io/blog/formula-bot/), [indiehackers interview](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/getting-serious-about-marketing-after-hitting-500k-arr-8XGnzrxG1neA3jy37x2D))

**Support burden: LOW–MEDIUM.** Mostly consumer one-shot purchases: refund requests and "my photos look weird" complaints, largely templatable. PhotoAI runs at 0 employees serving thousands of customers/mo. Quality-disappointment refunds are the main load.
**Distribution:** SEO (exact-match domains were decisive for PDF.ai and Formula Bot), affiliates (Submagic), TikTok/virality, audience. Highly automatable once ranking — but rankings are the battle.
**Pricing/WTP:** consumer one-time $19–59 (headshots: market band $15–50/session, vs. $230–350 studio — clear value math); prosumer subscriptions $15–35/mo (Formula Bot), $16–80/mo (Submagic).
**Time to first revenue:** fastest of all archetypes — days, if you catch a trend or rank.
**Failure modes:** (a) **foundation models absorbing the product** — Gemini "Nano Banana" now appears in 2026 "best headshot generator" lists, ChatGPT does free transcription/summarization; (b) one-time purchases mean you re-acquire revenue every month; (c) ad-dependence; (d) Damon Chen's PDF.ai is the cautionary tale — **revenue declining in 2025 because the space "was now crowded"** [SR].
**2026 saturation: HIGH.** The AI headshot market is ~$450–600M but lists 12+ funded competitors with $9–18 entry prices ([narkis.ai market analysis](https://narkis.ai/blog/the-ai-headshot-market-in-2026-size-growth-and-what-the-numbers-actually-mean), [miniloop.ai comparison](https://www.miniloop.ai/blog/best-ai-headshot-generator-2026)). The 2023 window that made PhotoAI/HeadshotPro/Submagic is closed; entering now means out-marketing incumbents with $300K/mo war chests.

### Archetype 4: AI chatbot/support automation for SMB websites (the "SiteGPT model")

**Named examples:** SiteGPT (**$20K MRR, 2 people, after a near-death 2024** — §1.1); Chatbase (**$10M ARR but 24–30 employees** [V]); incumbent pressure from Intercom Fin, Zendesk AI, Tidio at per-resolution pricing.
**Support burden: MEDIUM–HIGH — the irony of the category.** You sell support automation, but SMB buyers are non-technical: onboarding ("why doesn't it know my content?"), hallucination complaints, website-integration handholding. SiteGPT's 50% first-month churn is direct evidence of an expectation-vs-reality gap that generates tickets.
**Distribution:** free-tool SEO works (SiteGPT: 50+ free tools = 90% of traffic); the viral-launch channel of 2023 is gone.
**Pricing/WTP:** $39–259/mo flat (SiteGPT); $40→enterprise (Chatbase). Decent WTP because it substitutes labor.
**Time to first revenue / ceiling:** 2023 entrants got instant revenue; a 2026 entrant faces a crowded comparison-shopping market. Solo ceiling realistically $10–20K MRR (SiteGPT's actual trajectory), with high churn pressure.
**Failure modes:** churn (SMBs die and downgrade constantly), hallucination/quality risk, foundation models + website builders (Wix, Shopify) shipping native AI chat, per-resolution pricing wars from incumbents.
**2026 saturation: VERY HIGH.** This archetype's poster child (SiteGPT) nearly sold itself in distress in 2024. Not recommended as a fresh entry.

### Archetype 5: Programmatic SEO content/tool sites with subscriptions

**Named examples:** Formula Bot's free-tool constellation (§3 above — the *legitimate* version of pSEO: free tools feeding one subscription); SiteGPT's 50 free tools; PDF.ai's exact-match-domain SEO.
**The bad news is structural:** Google's scaled-content-abuse enforcement (March 2024 policy, escalated in the **March 2026 core update**) hit template-page sites with **50–90% traffic losses within two weeks**; one documented pSEO site lost ~93% of traffic overnight in April 2025 and took nearly a year to show a pulse ([digitalapplied.com](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/programmatic-seo-after-march-2026-surviving-scaled-content-ban), [seoalgorithmrecovery.com](https://seoalgorithmrecovery.com/scaled-content-abuse-recovery/), [thequerypost.com](https://thequerypost.com/seo-recovery-after-google-traffic-collapse/)). There's no reconsideration process for algorithmic hits. Add AI Overviews eating informational clicks, and *content*-pSEO is the most fragile archetype on this list.
**What still works [per recovery analyses]:** pages backed by genuinely unique structured data (live pricing, verified listings, proprietary datasets) and **free interactive tools** (calculators, converters, generators) — tool pages answer a query in a way AI Overviews can't fully replace.
**Support burden: NEAR-ZERO** (it's a website). **Distribution: was the whole point; now the main risk.**
**Pricing:** $9–29/mo subscriptions converting 1–3% of tool users.
**Failure modes:** single-platform dependency on Google; one core update = business over.
**2026 verdict:** dead as a standalone *content* strategy; alive as a *free-tool funnel* bolted onto archetypes 1–3.

### Archetype 6: Browser extensions / marketplace plugins (Chrome, Figma, Shopify)

**Named examples:**
- Chrome/VS Code: a dev.to author reports **$6,800/mo across 3 niche VS Code extensions** (Mar 2026; $5/mo pricing, 2–3% conversion, <4% churn) [SR, anonymous-ish — directional only] ([dev.to](https://dev.to/hopkins_jesse_cdb68cfa22c/how-i-make-6800month-selling-niche-vs-code-extensions-eji)); another runs **7 freemium Chrome extensions** via ExtensionPay+Stripe at only ~$650/mo target — illustrating the fat-tailed distribution [SR] ([dev.to](https://dev.to/ktg0215/monetizing-chrome-extensions-with-freemium-real-numbers-from-7-paid-extensions-5cj)). Industry synthesis: most extensions earn $0; indie tier $1–10K MRR; 70–85% margins; conversion <3% ([codetalenthub.io](https://www.codetalenthub.io/chrome-extensions-revenue-2026/)).
- Shopify: **ABConvert** $50K MRR in 24 months [SR]; **Kaching Bundle** 6-figure MRR portfolio (Erikas Mališauskas, [indiehackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/getting-out-of-the-freelancing-game-by-building-a-100k-mrr-shopify-app-portfolio-qdReVAgLjz6EpW4OrJSI)); counter-example: **ezInvoices — $735 total revenue in year one** despite 587 merchants and Built-for-Shopify status [SR] ([ezapps.io](https://ezapps.io/blogs/my-shopify-app-after-one-year)) — the honest median.
- Figma: **new-seller program is CLOSED** — you can't sell natively; you must run external license keys via Lemon Squeezy/Dodo ([forum.figma.com](https://forum.figma.com/ask-the-community-7/is-it-currently-impossible-to-sell-a-figma-plugin-yes-or-no-42302)). Typical paid-plugin revenue $150–800/mo, top tier $1–5K/mo [M — vendor blog estimates].

**Support burden: LOW–MEDIUM.** Utility extensions: near zero. Shopify apps: **merchant support expectations are high** and reviews (your ranking lifeblood) hostage you to fast replies — worst zero-ops fit of the three marketplaces.
**Distribution: the best part — marketplace organic search is genuinely automatable** (CWS/App Store SEO + reviews flywheel). Shopify additionally takes 15% over $1M lifetime, and rankings now favor incumbent review velocity ([taylorsicard.com](https://taylorsicard.com/blog/shopify-app-mvp-to-1m-arr)).
**Pricing/WTP:** consumer extensions $3–10/mo (low WTP); B2B/professional extensions $19–49+/mo; Shopify merchants $15–60/mo with usage upgrades.
**Time to first revenue:** fast (weeks) for extensions; Shopify ~3–6 months to first $1K MRR.
**Failure modes:** platform policy changes (Manifest V3 style), review-bombing, marketplace clones undercutting, the ezInvoices outcome (users ≠ revenue).
**2026 saturation:** Chrome generic-utility space saturated; **niche professional B2B extensions and Shopify revenue-generating apps (upsell/bundles/A-B testing) still produce new winners**.

### Archetype 7: Other promising zero-support models discovered

1. **Free-tool SEO funnel → one paid product** (the meta-pattern behind SiteGPT, Formula Bot, Bannerbear): ship 10–50 small free tools that each rank for a transactional query; funnel to one subscription. Survives the 2026 Google update *because tools, not content*. Lowest-cost distribution available to an audience-less founder.
2. **Boilerplates/codebases & info-products** (Marc Lou: ShipFast $1.3M+ lifetime; CodeFast ~$20K/mo [SR]): genuinely zero marginal support, but 100% audience-driven distribution — not viable without one.
3. **"Sell the pickaxe to AI agents":** Krasun and others note rising API demand from agent builders; developer-facing utilities consumed by AI agents (auth, browsing, screenshots, file conversion for agents) is the least saturated developer niche of 2026 [qualitative].
4. **Micro-acquisition instead of building** (Damon Chen bought Looseleaf.ai for $20K; Alex Le bought a Shopify app and grew it to $3.5K MRR [SR, [indieniche](https://indieniche.substack.com/p/he-acquired-the-app-then-grew-it)]) — mostly above your budget but possible at the $2–5K end for tiny assets.

## 4. Ranked recommendation for one person, 20 h/week, $2–7K, zero-human-ops, 2026

**#1 — Niche developer API / API-as-product (Archetype 1), ideally serving AI-agent builders.** Best support profile of any model (docs-deflected, technical customers), production lock-in creates real retention, infra cost fits the budget, and distribution (SEO + free dev tools + tutorials) is fully automatable on 20 h/wk. The trade-off is honesty about speed: plan for 12–18 months to $5–10K MRR (ScreenshotOne, Bannerbear, ScrapingBee all took that long). Mitigate commoditization by picking a niche job-to-be-done, pricing ≥$29–49 (ScrapingBee's lesson: cheap tiers = support load), and targeting the 2026 demand wave of agent-builders.

**#2 — Vertical AI micro-SaaS in a niche you already inhabit (Archetype 2).** The fastest verified paths to $14–25K MRR by unknown founders in 2024–25 (ChatSEO, LaunchFast) were both "productized own domain expertise + pre-existing channel into the niche." If you have a profession/community you genuinely know, this beats #1 on speed; if you don't, it drops below extensions, because the distribution can't be automated cold.

**#3 — Niche professional browser/marketplace extension with subscription (Archetype 6, Chrome or VS Code; Shopify only if you target merchant-revenue apps).** Lowest absolute risk and cost; marketplace organic is the only distribution channel that works from day one with zero audience. Realistic ceiling is modest ($1–7K/mo), making it a great first base-hit or portfolio component rather than the end-state. Avoid Figma (seller program closed) and be clear-eyed that the Shopify median outcome is ~$61/mo.

**#4 — Self-serve AI generation tool (Archetype 3) — only with a unique wedge.** The revenue ceilings are the highest in this report, but every big winner came from the closed 2023 window plus an audience or an exact-match domain costing more than your whole budget; PDF.ai's 2025 decline shows what crowding does even to winners. Enter only via an unserved language/format/profession wedge, with a free-tool SEO funnel (#7) as the distribution plan.

**#5 — Free-tool funnel (Archetype 7-1)** — not a business by itself; mandatory distribution layer for whichever of #1–4 you pick. Budget 30–50% of your 20 h/wk to it.

**#6 — SMB AI chatbot (Archetype 4): avoid.** Category poster child nearly died, churn is structurally brutal, and you compete with both $10M-ARR bootstrappers and Intercom/Zendesk in 2026.

**#7 — Programmatic SEO content subscriptions (Archetype 5): avoid as standalone.** The March 2026 core update made the core mechanism a policy violation; documented 60–93% overnight traffic losses with no appeal path.

**Cross-cutting reality check:** "zero-human-operation" is approximately true at the *margin* (each new customer adds ~zero work in archetypes 1, 6, 7), but no verified case shows zero founder hours — expect 3–10 h/wk on support+ops at $1–25K MRR even in the best archetypes, which fits inside your 20 h/wk. And the single biggest predictor of success across every verified case was not the product category but a pre-existing or cheaply acquirable distribution asset (niche membership, marketplace, free-tool SEO, or audience). Choose the archetype whose distribution asset you can realistically build — that is the actual decision.
